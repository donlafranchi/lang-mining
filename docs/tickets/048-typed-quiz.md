# 048 Typed answer in the Quiz
- Each due card has a type-the-English box with Check, or Show answer (as before).
- Checking: exact match on stored meanings, or "close" (plural/-ing/-ed endings, one typo in a word of 6+ letters). Close counts as right.
- Right: Good or Easy. Show answer: Again, Good or Easy (self-rated). Wrong typed answer: Again, or "I was right" (counts as Good, for synonyms we do not store).
- Same rules now used by Test me in lessons.
- Not built: English to target direction, handwriting, remembering "I was right" synonyms.
