# 053 Writing step in the Quiz
- Each Quiz card starts with "write it": the word is shown large (the model) and the learner copies it on a drawing pad with a finger or stylus (Undo, Clear). Then "Done, now translate" (enabled once something is written) or "Skip writing", then the typed translation as before.
- "Writing first: on/off" link above the card; stays for the visit only (resets on reload).
- Nothing is stored or checked; the movement is the point. Self-rating (Again/Good/Easy) is unchanged.
- Works for Spanish and Farsi (right-to-left word shown as written).
- Checked in a headless phone-size browser: strokes draw, Undo takes back one stroke, Clear empties.
- Not built: write from memory, writing in lessons or for sentences, remembering the on/off choice, stroke smoothing.
