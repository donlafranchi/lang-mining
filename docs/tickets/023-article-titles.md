# 023 Article titles

- Rename a lesson from its page (Rename under the title). Scoped to the signed-in person.
- The model also returns a short English title with each batch; the first batch's title becomes the lesson's title, but only while the lesson still has its stand-in title (start of the pasted text, or the site name). A page's own title and any rename are never overwritten.
- No SQL. Lessons built before this keep their titles.
